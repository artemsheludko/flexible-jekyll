Scientific and high-performance computing software.

Mike Heroux founded and led the Trilinos, Kokkos, Mantevo, HPCG, and E4S projects at Sandia National Laboratories while teaching at Saint John's University. Today he works on scientific software ecosystems, HPC-AI software futures, and generative AI in education through ParaTools, HPE, Hyperion Research, and the DOE PESO project.
